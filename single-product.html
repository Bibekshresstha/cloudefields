Dubai Chocolate Ice-Cream

Rich chocolate ice cream stacked with luxurious kunafa, coated in silky chocolate, and finished with a pistachio drizzle. A little crunchy, a little creamy — and completely irresistible.
NET 120 ml (4.0 fl oz)
ABOUT THIS ICE-CREAM

Inspired by Dubai's love for bold flavors and lavish desserts, this ice cream doesn't hold back.

We start with a deep, rich chocolate base—smooth, creamy, and indulgent from the first spoonful. It's coated in silky chocolate, then finished with a pistachio drizzle and layered with notes inspired by stacked kunafa textures.

The result? Decadent. Textural. Deeply satisfying.

This is chocolate that knows how to make an entrance—and stay unforgettable.

ALLERGEN INFORMATION:

This product contains wheat products, dairy products, soy products, and nut products.
